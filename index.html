About Suthar Research

A research-led platform built around a simple idea: good investing starts with understanding the business.

Suthar Research is an independent platform created by Pankaj Suthar, focused on equity research and long-term investing.

The approach combines fundamental analysis with a practical understanding of how businesses create value. The objective is not to predict every market move, but to study businesses carefully, assess risk and valuation, and develop a clear investment thesis.

Research published here is intended to help investors think more clearly and independently about listed companies and the markets.

For investors

Practical investing tips

Simple principles to use before buying a stock. The aim is better decision-making, not stock tips or guaranteed returns.

01

Know what you are buying

If you cannot explain how a company makes money, spend more time researching before investing.

02

Look beyond revenue growth

Check margins, cash flow, debt, return ratios and whether growth is actually creating shareholder value.

03

Do not ignore valuation

A great company can still be a poor investment when the market price assumes unrealistic growth.

04

Study the downside

Ask what could permanently damage the business and whether you can tolerate that risk.

05

Watch management and capital allocation

Track how management uses profits, raises capital, handles debt and treats minority shareholders.

06

Think in years, not days

Short-term prices are noisy. A strong investment thesis should be based on the business, not daily market movements.

A quick stock-buying checklist

Understand the business model
Check debt and cash flows
